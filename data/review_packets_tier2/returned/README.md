# Returned review packets

Drop your completed packet here with the SAME filename, `verdict` filled on every row
(`keep` or `strike`), plus any items you know I missed added at the end of the relevant bucket.

You do NOT need to wait for me to merge it. The return is your deliverable; the merge is
Architecture's, and it happens on the next Architecture wake rather than in a batch.

Reminders, in order of how often they get got wrong:

1. **Never strike a bucket for being frequent.** A bucket firing on most items is the
   most-demonstrated skill, not noise. Smith, 2026-08-11: getting the definite article right 571
   times is 571 pieces of evidence. Strike only what is WRONG.
2. **A generous list is safe; a wrong list is not.** An entry is judged only if the learner's answer
   engages it — engaged and right is a hit, engaged and wrong is a real miss, not engaged is a blank
   with no penalty. So an extra correct entry costs nothing, and an incorrect one makes the marker
   mark something that is not there.
3. **Add what I missed.** The proposals come from morph-it tagging of the reference translation. It
   sees morphology, not meaning, so it will have missed anything your construction expresses without
   a surface signature.
