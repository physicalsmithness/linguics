How this differs from v1

The right half is not a tab strip. v1 put themes, gender, spelling, accent and stress behind tabs in one panel — you'd see one at a time. Here themes own the top ~3/4 of the right half and the four drills keep a permanent reserved strip along the bottom that the themes scroll past. Nothing is hidden behind a tab, so every panel can zing on the same mark.
The rail stays — I had misread you. It is a scrub, not a range selector: one handle sliding the length of the corpus, and whichever thousand it passes over becomes the principal focus. Drag it in this mockup; the blocks re-rank as you go. Clicking a block still does the same thing, so there are two ways to the same act. The focused block wears its own “~2k–3k” label, and the “~” disposes of the 990-vs-1,000 mismatch I flagged: with an approximate label the block can stay 990 wide and nobody is misled.
Second-row furniture is in the top band. Strand tabs, drill chips and the whole scope bar share one row, which is what buys the height for two full-size left quadrants. The drill chips appear twice on purpose: as top-level chips here, and as “drill →” on each mini-panel, per your “maybe also next to their breakdowns”.
Nothing scrolls but the two things that must. The themes column (long by nature) and a long explanation inside the question card. Both are their own scrollboxes, so neither can move the question — that is the r126 rule applied by default rather than retrofitted.
