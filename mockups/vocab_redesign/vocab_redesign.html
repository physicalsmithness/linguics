What changed, and why

1 · The scope bar. Every filter is a chip that names its own provenance, and every chip has an ×. Today a selection made on the entry screen writes rankRanges and themes, while the vocab bar displays topN and subBand — different fields — so an entry-screen choice is structurally invisible, not merely unlabelled. The bar reads one state, so that cannot recur.
2 · Moving the view is not filtering. Today one click does both: the block handler sets vocabFilter.rankRange and refocuses. The drag rail moves the view; a separate “Practise these 990 words” button is the only thing that narrows the deck, and when pressed it appears as a frequency chip in the bar. Clicking a block still moves the view — it just no longer filters.
3 · Say what the chart is. A permanent subtitle: “the 18,000 most common words in Italian, commonest first.” Today that sentence exists only while you are looking at ranks 1–990; move the view and it is replaced by “Italian words by frequency, ranks 2001–2990”, which assumes you already knew.
4 · Scope is outlined in both panels at once. The frequency blocks inside scope carry a blue outline; the categories inside scope carry a wine outline and bold. Your two filters are visible in the two places they act, which is the “boxes around the section of frequency and the section of category” you asked for.
5 · The right column becomes categories, and scrolls. Themes, gender, spelling, accent and stress as tabs in one scrolling panel — so the second axis of analysis stops competing with the frequency grid for the same space. It is its own scrollbox (r126 gave it that), so scrolling it never moves the question.
6 · A visible way back. Clicking the brand mark already calls showEntry() — it works, you just can't see that it exists. “change on entry screen” in the scope bar says it in words.
